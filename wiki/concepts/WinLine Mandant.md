# WinLine Mandant

A **Mandant** is a **client / company** (set of company data) within a WinLine installation. All modules ([[WinLine FIBU]], [[WinLine KORE]], [[WinLine PPS]], …) operate on the currently selected Mandant and its data stand.

- **Mandantenwechsel** (client switch) lives on the MESONIC-Ribbon in the [[Mesonic WinLine]] shell (START).
- New-client setup follows an *Ablaufplan für eine Mandantenneuanlage*.
- Per-Mandant configuration is reached via **START → Optionen** (FIBU-Parameter, etc.) — see [[WinLine Settings]]; user/rights and system-level management live in [[WinLine ADMIN]].
