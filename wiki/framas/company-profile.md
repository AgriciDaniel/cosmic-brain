# Framas Company Profile

**Official Name**: Frantz Martz & Sons (Framas)  
**Industry**: High-performance footwear component manufacturing & technical development  
**Founded**: 1948  
**Headquarters**: Pirmasens, Germany  

## Overview

Framas is a global manufacturer and technical development partner specializing in high-performance components for the footwear industry. Originally founded by Franz Martz as a premier shoe last-maker, the company has evolved into a massive international operation.

### Historical Significance

- **1948**: Founded in Pirmasens, Germany by Franz Martz (originally "Dr. Lasts")
- **1930s**: Adi Dassler (Adidas founder) learned shoe handicraft from Franz Martz
- Pirmasens was historically Germany's shoe capital
- Evolved from small family business to international corporation
