# About Framas

Framas (officially Frantz Martz & Sons) is a global manufacturer and technical development partner specializing in high-performance components for the footwear industry.

Based on their company history and current operations, here is a breakdown of who they are and what they do:

⚙️ Core Business & Products
Footwear Technology: They develop and mass-produce high-precision molds, technical molded plastic components, and advanced manufacturing solutions for shoes.

Historical Roots: Founded in 1948 by Franz Martz (originally known as "Dr. Lasts"), the company started as a premier shoe last-maker in Pirmasens, Germany—which was then considered Germany's shoe capital. Notably, Adi Dassler (the founder of Adidas) even came to learn shoe handicraft from Franz Martz in the 1930s.

🌐 Global Reach
Framas has evolved from a small German family business into a massive international operation:

Team Size: Employs over 3,600 professionals globally across more than 20 nationalities.

Locations: They operate across 9 regions worldwide.

Manufacturing Hubs: They have future-oriented plastic component manufacturing facilities in Germany, Vietnam, Indonesia, and South Korea (Korea Vina).

Representative Offices: They maintain a presence in the USA, China, South Korea, Taiwan, and Hong Kong to ensure short communication channels with their brand partners.

🤝 Partners & Scale
The company acts as a behind-the-scenes engineering powerhouse for major athletic and footwear brands. They manage everything from the initial concept design to mass production, executing over 1,000 successful projects annually for more than 150 partners and customers.
