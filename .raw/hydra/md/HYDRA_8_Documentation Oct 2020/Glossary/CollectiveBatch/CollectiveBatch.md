Merged Batch

1  Merged Batch

Definition

A  merged  batch  is  a  special  kind  of  HYDRA  batch.  One  can  distinguish  a  merged  batch  from  a  non-

merged  batch,  as  the  merged  batch  is  assigned  1  -  n  batches.  The  merged  batch  "embraces"  the

assigned individual batches.

Merged  batches  are  especially  identified  in  the  system  and  with  respect  to  data,  they  have  a  special

relationship to the included individual batches.

Fields of application

Merged batches are used to improve logistical and systemic manageability of the collection of batches in

the  production  process.  Merged  batches  have  been  designed  to  embrace  individual  batches.

Consequently, by using merged batches and/or merged batch numbers, processes can be performed in

relation to the included individual batches, without having to perform them separately for each individual

batch.

Typical fields of application for merged batches are, among others:

  Collection of serial numbers

In this case, merged batches are used to be able to identify all of the included serial numbers.

  Packing/palletizing and stock transfer processes

Several  batches  are  combined  logically.  This  combination  may  then  be  relocated  or  used

elsewhere, once it has been identified.
