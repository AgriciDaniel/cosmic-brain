Batch

1  Batch

Synonyms

HYDRA batch, MES batch

Definition

A  batch  number  identifies  precisely  one  quantity  unit  of  a  product  which  can  be  considered  as  uniform

and  exists  precisely  once  at  a  single  location.  An  additional/different  quantity  unit  of  this  product  is

identified by a different batch number.
