Logistically Manageable Unit (LMU)

1  Logistically Manageable Unit (LMU)

Definition

The  logistically  manageable  unit  is  a  unit  that  can  be  managed  by  logistics.  This  unit  may  be  used

explicitly  (e.g.  transports  or  similar)  or  it  can  also  be  used  for  identification  purposes  in  IT  systems.

However, the logistically manageable unit does not have to be kept in all IT systems.
