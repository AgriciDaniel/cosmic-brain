Batch

1  Batch

Synonyms

Lot, ERP lot, ERP batch

Definition

A batch is the quantity unit of a product that can be considered homogeneous and that is identified by a

batch  number.  A  batch  may  be  stored  at  different  places  within  one  warehouse  and  in  different

warehouses at the same time.
