1  AIP-AddOn label printing

General

The  license  AIP-AED  upgrades  the  Acquisition  and  Information  Panel  (AIP)  with  functions  allowing  to

print production-related documents, such as time tickets, labels, material documents, routing slips, etc. at

shop floor terminals.

Compatibility list

The license AIP-AED can be used with the following printers:

  Zebra printer

o  Zebra P430 i

o  Zebra ZXP

o  Zebra Z6M

o  Zebra TLP 2844

  ETISYS

o  ETISYS EP-726

Please note: In general only printers are supported that can be operated with Windows printer drivers.
