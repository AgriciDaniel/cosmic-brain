1  Performance Analysis - General

1.1  General

It is the objective of the Performance Analysis to evaluate the data available from the connected systems.

Subject to the evaluation's objective, the following dashboards may be chosen.

Please note: MES Cockpit uses a separate data basis including the functions integrated there.

2  Performance Analysis

2.1  Dashboard: Order Evaluation

Function authorization  MC-OAnalysis

2.1.1.1

Order Overview Report

Tabular presentation of the order overview showing master data and the produced quantities including a

yield rate for the single orders. The yield rate is displayed as a linear pointer.

2.1.1.2

Site Evaluation Report

Up to six selected KPIs of the selected orders are shown and grouped by the site (system).

2.1.1.3

Order Evaluation Report

Up to six selected KPIs of the selected orders are shown as bar chart. In addition, the mean value of the

selected data is calculated and displayed.

2.1.1.4

RPA Analysis Report

The totals of individual RP accounts are presented for the selected orders in a pie chart.

Please note: If no values are available for individual RP accounts, they will neither be shown in

the diagram nor the legend.

2.1.1.5

Production Overview Report

The  production  overview  compares  the  entire  yield  and  scrap  quantities  (primary  quantity  unit)  of  the

selected orders.

2.1.1.6

Selections

The following selection options are provided in this dashboard:

  KPI

  Order numbers including site ID

  Header article (article/item of the order header)

  Customer number

2.2  Dashboard: Operation Evaluation

Function authorization  MC-OPAnalysis

2.2.1.1

Operation Overview Report

Tabular  presentation  of  the  operation  overview  showing  master  data  and  the  produced  quantities

including a yield rate for the single operations. The yield rate is displayed as a linear pointer.

The totals row shows the entire totals of yield and scrap quantities.

2.2.1.2

KPI Report

Up  to  three  specified  KPIs  of  the  selected  operations  are  shown  over  time.  In  addition,  the  arithmetic

mean value of selected data and KPIs is calculated and displayed.

2.2.1.3

Article-Related Evaluation/Report

Up to three specified KPIs of the selected operations are shown over time grouped by the article number.

In addition, the arithmetic mean value of selected data and KPIs is calculated and displayed.

2.2.1.4

Selections

The following selection options are provided in this dashboard:

  KPI

  Combined order and operation number including site ID

  Article

  Cost center

The following times/periods may be selected in this dashboard:

  Shift

  Day

  Month

  Quarter

  Year

  Calendar week
