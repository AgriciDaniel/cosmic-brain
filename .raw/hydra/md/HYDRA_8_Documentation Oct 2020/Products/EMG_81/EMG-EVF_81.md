Possible fields of application

Application service (AS) including functions for the assignment of recorded consumption values to objects

from shop floor data collection and materials management.

The function package is used if you would like:







to post consumption as material in relation to the produced articles/items or orders.

to perform evaluations/reports based on materials management

to analyze consumption in connection with the produced orders.

Integration

The licenses for the application services shop floor data/order data management (BDE-BDM) as well as

material  and  inventory  management  (MPL-MBV)  have  been  purchased.  Consequently,  the  posting  and

evaluation logics of the relevant services are available. MOC evaluation functions from the modules shop

floor  data  collection,  materials  management  and  energy  management  may  be  added.  With  respect  to

energy management, this is, for example, the function of the correlative consumption analysis EMG-KLE.

Functions

  Definition of "material types" for energy consumption

  Assignment of the "material" energy as consumption material within order data

  Consumption  recording  relating  to  orders  and  posting  based  on  the  energy  meters  assigned  to

the workplaces

  Listing  of  energy  consumption  relating  to  orders  based  on  the  consumption  that  has  been

recorded and posted as material

  Please  note:  Separate  licenses  have  to  be  purchased  for  evaluations/reports  used  as  MOC

functions from the modules.
