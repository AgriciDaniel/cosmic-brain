The dynamic modification history distinguishes between batch-related and characteristic-related histories.

Within  groupings  the  dynamic  modification  history  is  sorted  by  date  and  time  of  the  first  completion  of

inspection requirements.

The dynamic modification history is grouped and shown in tabular form. The dynamic modification history

based  on  characteristics  is  additionally  grouped  by  the  OP  sequence  number  of  the  inspection  step

characteristic.

Utilization

This application has been designed to verify the entire dynamic modification and present the respectively

determined inspection severity and the inspection result as basis of dynamic modification. In addition, the

current inspection severity may be corrected.

Prerequisite

The  dynamic  modification  history  only  considers  completed  inspection  requirements  that  have  been

modified dynamically based on characteristics and for which the first completion date, the first completion

time as well as the usage decision are assigned.

Selection criteria

Selection criteria are self-explanatory and not described separately.

Toolbar

Edit

The determined inspection severity may still be corrected by using the "edit" function.
