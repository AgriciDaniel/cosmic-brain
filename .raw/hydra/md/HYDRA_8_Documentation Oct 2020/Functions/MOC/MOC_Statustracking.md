Malfunction  tracking  is  the  ideal  tool  for  any  shift  foreman  and  person  responsible  for  production.  The

statuses of the individual machines are presented as bars in a clear, graphic presentation. The statuses

running  in  parallel  are  also  shown  as  a  drill-down  view.  In  the  event  of  failure  of  entire  lines,  it  is  also

possible to perform research into the causes via another detail application.

Usage

The machine time profile is an evaluation function in production management. The machine time profile is

used  for  the  timely  presentation  of  the  production  and  standstill  behavior  of  machines  in  the  foreman's

area  and/or  of  the  selected  line  over  a  specified  period  of  time. The  profile  shows  which  statuses  were

recorded at the individual machines at which point in time.

In  particular  for  line  analysis  purposes,  the  machines  causing  the  failure  of  the  central  aggregate  are

shown in addition to the machines of the line.
