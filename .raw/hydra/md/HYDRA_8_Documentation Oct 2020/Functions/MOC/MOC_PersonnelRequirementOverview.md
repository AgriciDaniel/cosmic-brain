The  overview  of  workforce  requirements  shows  the  requirements  for  people  based  on  the  planning  in

HLS. If operations are planned on a machine for which workforce requirements are configured, they are

displayed as workforce requirements.

<Screenshot>

Usage

Qualifications

The  planned  operations  are  displayed  per  workplace.  The  bar  layout  is  a  result  of  the  calculated

runtime  or  remaining  run  time  of  the  operations.  The  requirements  for  people  result  from  the

configuration  of  the  requirements  at  the  workplace.  The  available  people  who  have  the  required

qualification are listed with respect to the requirements.

Selection criteria

The following selection criteria are available in the application:

Qualification

The determination of the requirements can be limited to certain qualifications.

Planning profile

The determination of the workforce requirements can be limited using a planning profile. This field

can only be accessed if the additional function Advanced selection and visualization (PEP-ESV) is

present.

Period

Period for which the workforce requirements are to be determined.

By  selecting  a  qualification,  the  coverage  of  the  workforce  requirements  for  individual

qualifications  can  be  displayed.  Because  multiple  qualifications  can  be  assigned  to  one

employee,  it  is  also  required  to  check  the  workforce  requirements  without  limiting  the

qualification  in  order  to  list  the  number  of  available  employees  with  respect  to  the  workforce

requirements without taking the qualifications into consideration.

Detail applications

The right mouse button can be used to call the Workforce availability window in the histogram. The

position of the mouse specifies the point in time for which workforce availability is to be displayed.

In the Workforce availability window the people who are available and those who are unavailable at the
