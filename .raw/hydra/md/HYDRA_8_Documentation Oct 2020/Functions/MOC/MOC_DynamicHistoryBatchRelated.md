The dynamic modification history distinguishes between batch-related and characteristic-related histories.

Within  groupings  the  dynamic  modification  history  is  sorted  by  date  and  time  of  the  first  completion  of

inspection requirements.

The dynamic modification history is grouped and shown in tabular form. Groupings by the OP sequence

number  of  the  inspection  step  characteristic  are  not  performed  for  batch-related  dynamic  modification

history.

Utilization

This  application  has  been  designed  to  verify  the  entire  dynamic  modification  and  to  present  the

respectively determined inspection severity and the inspection result as basis of dynamic modification. In

addition, the current inspection severity may be corrected.

Prerequisite

The  dynamic  modification  history  only  considers  completed  inspection  requirements  that  have  been

modified dynamically based on batches and for which the first completion date, the first completion time

as well as the usage decision are assigned.

Selection criteria

Selection criteria are self-explanatory and not described separately.
