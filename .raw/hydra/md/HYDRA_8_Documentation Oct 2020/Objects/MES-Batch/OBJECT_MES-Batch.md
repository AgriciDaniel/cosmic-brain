Batch Object

1  Batch Object

Definition

A batch is a homogeneous quantity of a material of the same quality that is located at one and the same

place at one point in time.

A  batch's  granulation  and/or  the  batch  size  depend  on  the  corresponding  material  and  is  in  general

geared to containers or transport units. To track individual parts, the batch size 1 is selected.

Usage

Batches are used to assess, to control and to evaluate by tracking material flows in the production of a

company.

They can also be used to precisely group and identify quantities of a material in case of external sending-

ahead (e.g. transport to the customer).

Structure

Each  batch  is  identified  by  a  unique  batch  identification  that  is  either  assigned  by  an  upstream  system

(mostly an ERP system) or by the system itself that is administered in the system; and which is physically

Integration

Batches  can  be  posted  to  a  material  buffer  in  order  to  determine  and  to  monitor  the  inventories  in

production.

A  material  type  is  assigned  to  each  batch  that  includes  essential  control  indicators  depending  on  the

batch's situation (input or output batch).

To enter logical transport resources a transport unit can be assigned to each batch.

Batches can be reserved for an order/ operation.
