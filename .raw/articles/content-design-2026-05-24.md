# Content Design - Fluent 2 Design System

Words are design material alongside iconography and illustration. "What you say, when you say it, and how are all important factors."

## Designing Content — Three Core Questions
1. **Who is your audience?**
2. **What do they want to accomplish?**
3. **How might they feel?**

## Writing Content — Core Principles

- **Keep it simple** — plainly structured language; short sentences and fragments
- **Get to the point fast** — make choices obvious
- **Talk like a person** — informal, conversational, one-on-one tone

## Best Practices

- **Present tense** — easier to understand
- **Active voice** — direct, people-focused
- **Capitalization**: sentence-case on Windows/Android/web; title-case on iOS/macOS
- **Write simply** — translate jargon
- **Use headings** to organize
- **Second person** — "you, your, yours" fosters friendly tone
- **Punctuation**: always use question marks; periods only after complete sentences; minimize exclamations; minimize punctuation in UI (headers, buttons, lists)

## Accessibility / Global Readiness

- **Link text**: short, descriptive; avoid "Click here"
- **Avoid directional terms**: no "above," "below," "right," "left"
- **Screen reader**: provide alt text
- **Logical organization**: headings, tables, lists
