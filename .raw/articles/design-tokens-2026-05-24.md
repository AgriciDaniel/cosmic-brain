# Design Tokens - Fluent 2 Design System

## Overview

"Design tokens are stored values used to assign Fluent styles like color, typography, spacing, or elevation, without hardcoding pixels and hex codes."

## Global Tokens

Foundation layer. Context-independent raw values: hex codes, typography, border radius, stroke width, animation properties.

## Alias Tokens

Secondary layer. Semantic meaning over raw values. For complex elements (shadows, typography), aliases consolidate multiple values into single accessible formats.

### Naming Structure

Naming makes intended application immediately apparent. Designers understand where and how to apply consistent styling without numerical lookups.

## Theming

Architecture prioritizes flexibility + accessibility. Natively accommodates light, dark, and high-contrast modes plus branded variations. Adequate contrast throughout.
