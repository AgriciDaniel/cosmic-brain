## Styling Text

### Casing
Sentence case. Avoid all caps ("difficult to read").

### Alignment
Baseline for vertical rhythm. Left-align LTR; right-align RTL. Center selectively.

### Color
Standard text: ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. Large text (>18.5px bold or 24px regular): ≥ 3:1.
