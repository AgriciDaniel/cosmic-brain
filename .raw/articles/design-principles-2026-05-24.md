# Design Principles - Fluent 2 Design System

Design principles serve as foundational values that express shared vision and guide decision-making. They influence how product designers approach and solve problems. The Fluent principles are grounded in understanding how customers navigate the world and reflect beliefs about empowering people.

## Natural on Every Platform

You want to know what to do. Experiences should adapt to the device you're on and build off the familiar—designing for what you already understand.

**Functional aspect:** "A layout that adapts to different screen sizes and is aware of the platform it's on makes an experience feel natural." This approach enables reusing native platform components and patterns about 80% of the time, allowing focus on signature experiences.

**Emotional aspect:** Experiences become intuitive and expected, creating reliability and trust.

## Built for Focus

You want to stay in the flow. Experiences should inspire action, drawing you forward simply and seamlessly.

**Functional aspect:** "When technology communicates and performs it enables people to do what they want on their terms." Products shouldn't obstruct user goals.

**Emotional aspect:** Reduced visual clutter keeps people centered, calm, and confident.

## One for All, All for One

You want to be included. Experiences should consider, learn, and reflect diverse perspectives and abilities.

**Functional aspect:** Including varied perspectives early in design creates better solutions and enables creative problem-solving through constraints.

**Emotional aspect:** Inclusion creates belonging.

## Unmistakably Microsoft

You want to recognize what you're looking for. Experiences should feel unified—one moment, one product, one experience at a time.

**Functional aspect:** Focus on signature experiences connecting products with distinctive Microsoft identity through color, sound, illustration, and icons.

**Emotional aspect:** Personality strengthens connection and recognition.
